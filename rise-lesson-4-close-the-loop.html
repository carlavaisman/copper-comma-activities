Close the loop

The conversation ends, but the case keeps going. A good case note lets the next person pick up exactly where you left off.

About 5 minutesObjective 4Job aid included

The note is for the next person

Imagine Dana contacts Northline again next week. A different agent opens her case, and everything they know about the first conversation comes from what you wrote.

If the note says “fixed it,” they’ll have to ask Dana to explain everything again, and that second explanation is often where a frustrated customer becomes a lost one.

If the next agent has to ask the customer to repeat themselves, the note wasn’t finished.

Four parts of every case note

Northline’s policy asks for the same four parts every time. Turn each card to see what belongs there.

Compare two notes

Both notes describe Jordan’s conversation with Dana from Lesson 2. Only one would help the next agent.

Cust upset re: double charge. Fixed it. Will follow up.
  • Issue: Upset about which charge? Was the account verified? Was the duplicate confirmed?
  • Evidence: What records were checked, and what did they show?
  • Action: What does “fixed it” mean? There’s no reversal reference.
  • Owner/update: Who will follow up, and by when?
IssueCustomer (NL-48213, authenticated) reported a duplicate March charge. Duplicate confirmed. EvidenceORD-7730: 1 monthly plan purchase. 2 × $24.00 payments on Mar 3, both Settled. ActionReversed the duplicate payment under the duplicate-charge policy. Ref REV-20931. OwnerJ. Okafor. Confirmation email to customer by 5:00 PM ET, Mar 3.

The strong note is only a few lines longer. The difference is that it swaps vague words (upset, fixed, will follow up) for details the next person can check: an account, a record, a reference number, a name, and a time.

i
Write facts rather than feelings. “Customer upset” tells the next agent nothing they can use. “Customer reported a duplicate March charge” does.

Your turn: write the note

Write a case note for the conversation below. You’ll then compare it with a model note and review it yourself.

Case: Ana Lima · NL-60318

  • Account authenticated through the approved process.
  • Ana says she was billed twice in June.
  • Order ORD-9112: one monthly plan purchase.
  • Transactions: two $24.00 payments on Jun 2, both Settled.
  • You reversed the duplicate (ref REV-31877) and told Ana you’ll email her a confirmation by 4:00 PM ET today.

Use your own initials as the owner. At least 40 characters.

Northline · Support job aid

Acknowledge · Verify · Resolve · Record

Billing concerns
1Acknowledge
Name the impact on the customer. Move toward the evidence. Don’t promise anything yet.
2Verify
Authenticate through the approved process. Never ask for a password or a full card number. Check the order record and each payment’s status.
3Resolve
One purchase + two settled payments: you may reverse the duplicate.
Pending authorization: explain it, say what you’ll check, and set an update time.
Extra compensation or exception: prepare a supervisor handoff and don’t promise approval.
4Record
Verified issue · Evidence consulted · Action or escalation · Owner and next-update time.

Designer’s note · instructional strategy and native build plan

Purpose

Objective 4: write a concise case note that records evidence, action, and next steps. The case note is the course’s most transferable on-the-job behavior and the most practical thing to measure in a pilot, for example by checking how complete real notes are.

Design decisions

  • Write it for the next reader. The lesson opens with the agent who reads the note later, which gives learners a concrete reason the note matters beyond compliance.
  • Contrasting cases. Weak and strong notes about the same conversation, so the only thing that differs is the quality of the note.
  • Honest self-review. Free text isn’t scored automatically for meaning. Learners compare their note with a model and check four criteria themselves. If they leave a box unchecked, they’re prompted to revise.
  • Job aid as the takeaway. The four-step structure from the whole course is condensed onto one page for use on the job.
