    Designer’s note · branching design, scoring logic, and native build plan

    Purpose

    This prototypes the practice stage intended for embedding in Rise Lesson 3. It covers all four objectives across three cases, and guidance fades with each one. Case 1 is scaffolded step by step. Case 2 gives a single, less guided decision in which learners have to open the record themselves. Case 3 is the final challenge, with critical scoring and a case-note task.

    Branching and feedback

    • Choices change what the customer says next, the customer’s state (Frustrated, Guarded, Calmer, Satisfied), and what goes into the case file.
    • Every distractor is a plausible workplace error. Feedback names the error, explains its consequence in the conversation, and cites the policy, followed by a focused retry of that decision only.
    • The Case 2 debrief identifies the type of evidence-versus-assumption error the learner made, including whether they answered before opening the record.

    Critical scoring

    Extra clicks can’t make up for an unsafe remedy. In Case 3, an incorrect critical decision sets CriticalError, shows targeted feedback, and requires a fresh attempt at that decision. PracticeComplete is set only after all three cases are resolved and the case-note self-review is finished. The case note is reviewed by the learner and isn’t scored automatically for meaning.
